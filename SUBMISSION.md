## 🧪 4. Evaluation Credentials & Persona Journeys
Velora features an intentional, built-in **Role-Based Evaluation Workspace** designed specifically for frictionless hackathon grading. Judges do **not** need to register accounts or memorize credentials.

By clicking **"Select Persona"** on the landing page header, you can instantly assume any of the three core enterprise roles to evaluate the corresponding workflow:

### 👤 Persona 1: Employee Journey (Priya Sharma)
*   **Role:** Software Engineer
*   **Department:** Engineering
*   **Key Journeys to Test:**
    1.  Create and submit a goal sheet for the active FY 2026-27 cycle.
    2.  Add individual goals with specific Units of Measure (UoMs) and weights.
    3.  Log quarterly progress updates and achievements during the Q1 check-in phase.

### 👥 Persona 2: Manager Journey (Vikram Mehta)
*   **Role:** Engineering Manager
*   **Department:** Engineering
*   **Key Journeys to Test:**
    1.  Access the **Approvals Portal** to review submitted goal sheets for direct reports (e.g., Priya Sharma).
    2.  Provide line-by-line inline feedback, request rework, or lock goal sheets with a single click.
    3.  Conduct quarterly reviews and score team performance metrics.

### 🛡️ Persona 3: HR Admin Journey (Admin User)
*   **Role:** HR Administrator
*   **Department:** Human Resources
*   **Key Journeys to Test:**
    1.  Create and manage performance cycles, toggle cycle phases (Drafting, Approval, Mid-Year Check-in, Closed).
    2.  Publish global shared organization goals (KPIs) to specific departments.
    3.  View real-time compliance audit logs recording all state modifications across the portal.
